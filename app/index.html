    Road to the platinum — about 200 hours, one playthrough

    No online trophies, no difficulty setting, no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start and the end of every chapter (15 slots, one autosave) and you cannot lock yourself out of anything except the single note in Stage 1.

    1. Stage 1 — The story, 60–80 hours

      Play the five chapters through and enjoy them. Do the optional missions as they appear, scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app and study new animals through the binoculars as you go — all of it counts toward the 100% checklist later. There are no mission gold medals to worry about.

      Watch out: one meditation in Chapter 1, at Jason’s house after Down to Earth, can only be done in Chapter 1. Find Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept when she offers to teach you. It is still unconfirmed whether it is required for Positive Mental Attitude, so do it while you can.

    2. Stage 2 — The 100% Completion Checklist, about 100 hours

      After the story, open Progress, Stats, 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% true completion: 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 card sets, 100 WAiNK scans, 33 underwater discoveries, 100 Cocaine Flamingos and any 100 optional missions. The only parts that demand everything are the robberies (39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 Payday Loan jobs, 8 bank vaults), the shooting ranges, the drone races and Bret’s 15 Target Decks.

    3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and questline finals, about 10 hours

      Finish the questlines for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret, and the 8 stranger strands that count. Every trophy-related quest giver is still around after the story, so nothing here is missable.

    4. Stage 4 — Cleanup and the platinum

      Mop up what is left: the UFO easter egg, the tank, the bowling alley valuables, the showroom Banshee, the marked bills and the garages. When the last trophy pops, Vice Got a Price! follows at once.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy is potentially missable. Everything else can be finished after the story in free-roam.

    Positive Mental Attitude Bronze

    Meditate at every spot across Leonida. One meditation, at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, is only available during that chapter: after the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept her offer to teach you. It raises the meditation counter but is not marked as a spot, so it is still unconfirmed whether it is required for the trophy. The 100% checklist only needs 15 of 18 meditations, so it does not matter there. Do it in Chapter 1 and the question goes away.

    Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap

    Two more honest unknowns: the method for Bad Luck, I Guess is still unconfirmed (the working theory ties it to the Payday Loans store from the prologue, in Little Cuba), and which exact animal pops Fair Game is still unconfirmed. Both guides in the checklist say so plainly instead of guessing.
