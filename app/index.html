Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, one playthrough, no mission replay. Difficulty 4/10. Work the stages in order — most of stage 2 happens naturally while you play stage 1, and stage 4 is just whatever is left.

100% Completion Checklist — the thresholds

Unlocks after the story under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% of the game's true content, not everything. Nothing on it is missable.

    Missables

    Good news first: there is only one trophy that can be missed, and everything else stays available in free-roam after the story.

    Save-slot habit that costs nothing

    The game gives you 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave, and there is no mission replay. Keep a save at the start and the end of each chapter (10 saves), then rotate the remaining 5 for free-roam cleanup. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off for a trophy run.
