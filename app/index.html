Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, one playthrough, no mission replay and no difficulty setting. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter — there are 15 manual save slots — because there is no mission replay.

The 100% Completion Checklist

It opens after the story at Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion — progress made during the story already counts. The bar is lenient, closer to 60% true completion: any 100 optional missions, any 70 of the 80 challenges, 300 of the 397 stunts, 200 of the 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of the 255 animals studied, plus all robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests. Nothing on the checklist is missable.

Missables

Only one trophy in the whole list is potentially missable. Everything else — story trophies included — can still be earned after the story in free-roam.

Nothing else can be missed

Optional missions do disappear after a while and some quest givers go temporarily unavailable, but every trophy-related character comes back later, and all trophy-related questlines stay available after the story. A few magazine collectibles sit in story buildings that close, but they also spawn elsewhere and are not needed for any trophy. So the only thing to watch for is the Chapter 1 meditation above.
